# Levy accounts

## Arrears
A levy account is in arrears when the balance is unpaid past the due date.
Arrears are reported in whole Rand.

## Payment arrangements
Debit order arrangements and partial settlements are both permitted. A promise
to pay requires a specific amount and a specific date.
